\section{Getting started}

IronMan runs the whole ironing business in one place: customers book a
pickup, staff track every garment through the hub, riders deliver and
collect payment, and the office manages stock, invoices and cash.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Who} & \textbf{Where to go} \\
\midrule
Office staff & \texttt{console.ironmanindia.co} \\
Riders (field staff) & \texttt{ironmanindia.co/field} — open on a phone \\
Customers & \texttt{ironmanindia.co} $\rightarrow$ \menu{Schedule a Pickup} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Logging in}
Enter your email and password and press \menu{Log in}. The menu on the
left only shows the screens your role can use.

\screen[0.62]{01-login}

\callout{\textbf{Demo environment logins} (all use the password
\texttt{IronMan@2026}): \texttt{founder@ironman.test},
\texttt{admin@ironman.test}, \texttt{operator@ironman.test},
\texttt{field@ironman.test}. These accounts exist only where the demo data
has been loaded. Never use this password on real accounts.}

\subsection{About the demo data}
The demo set contains one hub, six apartments, 24 customers and 44 orders
spread across every stage, plus stock, invoices, payments and cash
handovers. Because it is generated in one go, many timeline entries share
the same time. You can create, edit and pay for anything freely.

\section{Daily operations}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{How an order moves}
\textit{Scheduled $\rightarrow$ Pickup assigned $\rightarrow$ Picked up $\rightarrow$ At hub $\rightarrow$ Intake verified
$\rightarrow$ Pressing $\rightarrow$ Ready $\rightarrow$ Out for delivery $\rightarrow$ Delivered $\rightarrow$ Closed.}
Each step is recorded with who did it and when. Problems (damage, missing
item, failed pickup) are raised as \emph{exceptions} without stopping the
order.

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Dashboard}
Today at a glance: open orders, late pickups, deliveries and bookings.
Click any order to open it.
\screen{02-dashboard}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Orders}
\menu{Orders} lists every order with its stage and amount, with search
and filters. For a customer dropping clothes at the shop, press
\menu{New order}, pick the customer and service, set the garment counts
and press \menu{Create order}. The estimate comes from the current price
list and is corrected when the hub counts the garments at intake.
\screen{03-orders}
\screen[0.8]{05-new-order}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Order details}
Everything about one order: items, the bag and each garment's stage,
customer, invoice and payments. Use \menu{Report an issue} to raise an
exception, and \menu{Print tag} to label a bag.
\screen{04-order-detail}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Production}
The hub's work in progress by stage. Click a stage tile to filter the
list; scan a bag tag to move it forward.
\screen{06-production}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Exceptions and route days}
\menu{Exceptions} is the queue of damaged, missing and disputed items,
each with a severity, an owner and an SLA showing whether it is on track
or overdue.
\menu{Route Days} (Admin and Founder) assigns the day's pickups and
deliveries to riders.
\screen[0.8]{09-exceptions}
\screen[0.8]{10-route-days}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Customers}
Search customers, open one to see their addresses, notes, store credit and
where they came from (\emph{Acquired via}).

\callout{\textbf{Lifetime orders} and \textbf{lifetime spend} count
\emph{delivered} orders only. A new customer whose first order is still in
progress shows 0 and the status \emph{Lead} — this is expected.}
\screen{08-customer-detail}

% ── 4 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\needspace{11cm}
\section{Supplies (inventory)}

\menu{Supplies} tracks hangers, covers, bags and chemicals. A yellow
banner lists items at or below their reorder level.

\begin{itemize}
\item \menu{Receive stock} — record a delivery from a supplier, with quantity and unit cost.
\item \menu{Adjust stock} — record items used, wasted, returned, or a recount.
\item \menu{New item} — add a stock item with its reorder level.
\end{itemize}
Stock is also taken automatically when a garment is packed, using the
consumption rules below.
\screen{11-supplies}

Admins and Founders also see two more tabs. \menu{Movements} is the full history of every stock change, with
who made it, and \menu{Consumption rules} sets what each garment uses
(for example, one hanger and one cover per shirt).
\screen{12-supplies-movements}
\screen{13-supplies-rules}

Operators see stock and can receive and adjust it, but not costs, the
history or the rules.
\screen{21-operator-supplies}

% ── 5 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\needspace{11cm}
\section{Billing}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Invoices and receivables}
An invoice is created automatically when an order is delivered.
\menu{Invoices} shows every invoice with what is still owed. Filter by
customer, status or date, or tick \menu{Outstanding only} to see who owes
money. Admins and Founders can \menu{Export CSV} for the accountant.

If an invoice could not be created automatically, the order appears in a
yellow \emph{delivered without an invoice} panel at the top. Open the
order and press \menu{Issue invoice}.
\screen{14-invoices}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Payments, credit notes and write-offs}
Open an invoice to download the PDF and:
\begin{itemize}
\item \menu{Record payment} — cash, UPI or store credit; part payments are fine.
\item \menu{Issue credit note} (Admin, Founder) — reduce what the customer owes.
\item \menu{Write off} (Admin, Founder) — close a balance that will not be collected.
\end{itemize}
If a credit note is issued after the customer has paid, the invoice shows
\emph{Refund due}.
\screen{15-invoice-detail}

\needspace{11cm}
\subsection{Cash}
Riders hand collected cash to the hub; \menu{Cash} (Admin, Founder) shows
each handover, any shortfall, and deposits to the bank.
\screen[0.85]{16-cash}

% ── 6 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\needspace{11cm}
\section{Pricing, partners and messages}

\menu{Pricing} (Founder only) holds price lists per garment and offers;
other roles see a restricted notice.
\menu{Partners} (Admin, Founder) registers watchmen and influencers and
issues their referral codes. A customer who books with a code is credited
to that partner automatically.
\menu{Notifications} shows every message the system has queued for
customers.
\screen[0.8]{18-pricing}
\screen[0.8]{17-partners}

% ── 7 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\needspace{11cm}
\section{Field app (riders)}

Riders open \texttt{ironmanindia.co/field} on their phone and log
in. \menu{Jobs} lists today's pickups and deliveries. A job shows the
customer, address and a \menu{Open in Maps} button; the rider scans the bag
and marks it done, or reports a problem. \menu{Cash} shows cash in hand
and lets the rider \menu{Hand over cash} to a hub staff member, who
confirms the amount received; any shortfall is recorded against the
rider. Actions taken without signal
are saved and sent when the phone is back online.
\phones{24-field-jobs}{25-field-job}{26-field-cash}

% ── 8 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\needspace{11cm}
\section{Customers}

Customers book from \texttt{ironmanindia.co} in seven short steps:
pincode, address, service, garment counts, pickup slot, phone check and
confirm. They can enter a referral code or say how they heard about us.
Every order has a tracking link that needs no login, and customers can
sign in to see order history, addresses and invoices.
\phones{28-book-start}{29-tracking}{30-account-signin}

\callout{\textbf{Not yet live for real customers:} signing in and booking
need a code sent to the customer's phone. Text-message sending is not
connected yet, so these steps can only be tried in the demo environment.}

\section{Not in this build yet}

\item GST tax invoices — waiting on the business's GST details.
\item WhatsApp and SMS messages to customers, including login codes.
\item Online payments (cash and UPI at the door are recorded today).
\item Partner commissions and payouts, campaigns and marketing spend.
\item iPhone and Android apps (the web works on phones today).
\end{itemize}

\needspace{11cm}
\section{Reporting a problem}
Send a screenshot with: the screen, your role, the time, and the order or
invoice number if there is one. Every action in IronMan is logged with
the person and time, so this is enough for us to trace it.
